Our Story

Four Decades In The Fold

Vijson Box is a packaging house based in Ludhiana, Punjab — at the heart of India's textile and hosiery manufacturing belt. For more than 45 years, we've made one thing our craft: boxes that carry your goods and your brand with equal care.

Over the decades, that focus has earned the trust of 100+ businesses across the textile and hosiery trade — built order by order, on fair dealing and boxes that show up right, every time.

  • 45+ years of hands-on packaging experience
  • 100+ textile & hosiery businesses served
  • Based in Ludhiana — close to Punjab's textile hub
  • High-quality printing at low, fair pricing
  • Experienced, skilled labour force on every order
  • Professional offset machinemen with years of expertise
